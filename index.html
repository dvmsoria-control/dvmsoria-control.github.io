pipeline-videos

pipeline-videos is a private, personal tool. It assembles narrated documentary videos for its owner's own YouTube channel and saves the finished video files to the owner's own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What the app accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file. With it, the app can see and change only the files and folders that the app itself creates in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot open, read, change or delete any other file in Drive.

What the app does with that access

It uploads finished video files (MP4) to a folder it created in the owner's Google Drive. Nothing else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read, collect or store any other personal data.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • It does not use Google user data to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Storage and security

The access credentials are kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's private code repository and are used only to upload the owner's own videos.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Contact

Questions about this app can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
